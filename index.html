QUESTIONS / ANSWERED

The practical stuff.

Still need help? Write to support@civilcalcpro.com.

Does CivilCalc Pro work without internet?

Yes. The calculators run on-device. Internet is only needed for optional features such as Google Drive backup, Google Play purchases, and ads in the free tier.

Do I need to create an account?

No. CivilCalc Pro has no separate account system. Google sign-in is only used if you choose to enable Drive backup.

Can I share results with my team?

Yes. Export a clean PDF report or a CSV for spreadsheets, then share it through the standard Android share sheet.

What is included in the free tier?

Most calculators are available free with occasional ads. A paid upgrade removes ads and unlocks the complete set without rewarded-ad gates.

Is it a substitute for a licensed engineer?

No. CivilCalc Pro is an estimation and workflow tool. Always verify outputs against project drawings, local codes, and qualified professional review.
