QUESTIONS / ANSWERED

The practical stuff.

Still need help? Write to support@civilcalcpro.com.

Does CivilCalc Pro work offline without internet?

Yes. All 320+ civil engineering, construction quantity, RCC steel, electrical, and unit conversion calculators run offline directly on your Android device. Internet is only used for optional Google Drive backup, Google Play billing verification, and free-tier ads.

What calculators are included in CivilCalc Pro?

CivilCalc Pro includes over 320 calculators across 7 core categories: Concrete & Staircases, Steel & RCC Reinforcement (BBS), Quantity & Material Take-Off (brickwork, plaster, tiles, paint, earthwork, formwork), Electrical & MEP Sizing, Area & Land Measurement, Tank & Geometric Volume, and Engineering Unit Conversions, plus multi-item Project BOQs.

Can I generate BOQ reports and export PDF or CSV files?

Yes. Save individual calculations into named site projects to aggregate cement, sand, aggregate, steel, masonry, and labour costs into a clean Bill of Quantities (BOQ), then export or share formatted PDF reports and spreadsheet-ready CSV files.

Do I need to create an account to use CivilCalc Pro?

No. CivilCalc Pro has no mandatory sign-up or cloud account. Projects, history, custom material rates, and defaults stay on your phone by default, with optional Google Drive backup under your own account.

Does CivilCalc Pro support both metric (m, mm) and imperial (ft, ft/in) units?

Yes. Dimension inputs support millimetres (mm), centimetres (cm), metres (m), inches (in), decimal feet (ft), and compound feet-inches (ft/in), alongside regional cement bag weights, custom mix ratios, and 25 languages.

Is CivilCalc Pro a substitute for a licensed structural engineer?

No. CivilCalc Pro is an estimation and field workflow toolkit. Always verify outputs against approved structural drawings, applicable building codes, and qualified professional review.
