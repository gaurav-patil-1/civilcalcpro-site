COMMON QUESTIONS

Field FAQ.

Need support or want to request a calculator? Email support@civilcalcpro.com.

How many CFT and CUM are in 1 Brass of sand or aggregate?

1 Brass equals 100 cubic feet (100 CFT), which converts to approximately 2.8317 cubic metres (CUM). CivilCalc Pro converts between Brass, CFT, CUM, and metric tonnes instantly.

How do you measure an irregular 4-sided land plot accurately?

Measure all four boundary sides plus at least one diagonal on site. CivilCalc Pro splits the quadrilateral into two triangles using Heron’s formula to give the exact plot area-avoiding the classic average-opposite-sides error.
