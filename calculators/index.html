COMMON QUESTIONS

Field FAQ.

Need support or want to request a calculator? Email support@civilcalcpro.com.

Are all 320+ calculators available offline on construction sites?

Yes. Every calculation engine runs locally on your Android phone with zero internet dependency, so you can estimate concrete, steel, masonry, and electrical loads in basements or remote sites.

Can I switch between feet-inches (ft/in) and metric (mm, m) on any input?

Yes. Length, span, thickness, spacing, and diameter inputs support mm, cm, m, in, ft, and compound ft/in, converting seamlessly without manual unit math.

How does CivilCalc Pro handle wastage and custom material rates?

Each quantity calculator exposes editable wastage percentages, dry-volume multipliers, cement bag weights, and optional unit prices so your project BOQ reflects real local procurement rates.
