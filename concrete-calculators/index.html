COMMON QUESTIONS

Field FAQ.

Need support or want to request a calculator? Email support@civilcalcpro.com.

How is dry concrete volume calculated from wet volume?

CivilCalc Pro multiplies net wet concrete volume (plus optional wastage) by the dry-volume factor-1.54 by default to account for voids in sand and coarse aggregate-and divides by the sum of mix proportions to compute cement, sand, and aggregate quantities.

Does the trapezoidal footing calculator use the exact frustum formula?

Yes. Sloped/trapezoidal footings sum the rectangular base cuboid with the exact prismoidal/frustum wedge volume H/6 × [A1 + A2 + 4·Am] (or equivalent exact cross-sectional integration) rather than crude average-area approximations.

Can I change cement bag weight and mix proportions?

Yes. Standard grades (such as 1:1.5:3 for M20 or 1:1:2 for M25) as well as custom ratios and bag weights (50 kg, 40 kg, 25 kg, or 94 lb) can be customized per calculation or saved in Defaults.
