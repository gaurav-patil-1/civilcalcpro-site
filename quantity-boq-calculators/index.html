COMMON QUESTIONS

Field FAQ.

Need support or want to request a calculator? Email support@civilcalcpro.com.

Can I customize brick dimensions and mortar joint thickness?

Yes. Whether your region uses modular bricks (190 × 90 × 90 mm), traditional bricks (230 × 110 × 75 mm), US modular bricks, or 600 × 200 mm AAC blocks, you can set exact unit dimensions and joint thickness.

What formats can CivilCalc Pro export for client or procurement sharing?

CivilCalc Pro exports formatted PDF project & BOQ reports for clients/supervisors as well as structured CSV files that open directly in Excel or Google Sheets.

Does project data stay private on my phone?

Yes. Saved projects, rates, and BOQs are stored locally on your device by default, with optional user-controlled backup to your own Google Drive.
