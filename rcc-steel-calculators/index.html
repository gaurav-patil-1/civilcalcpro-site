COMMON QUESTIONS

Field FAQ.

Need support or want to request a calculator? Email support@civilcalcpro.com.

What formula does CivilCalc Pro use for steel bar weight?

For steel density 7850 kg/m³, cross-sectional area π·D²/4 gives unit weight D² / 162.28 kg/m (commonly written D²/162 where D is bar diameter in mm). You can also customize material density for stainless steel, aluminium, brass, or copper.

Are hook lengths and bend deductions included in stirrup cutting length?

Yes. Column and beam ring calculators deduct concrete cover to the bar centreline/outer face, add configurable hook lengths, and apply 90° and 135° bend deductions so bar benders get accurate cut lengths.

Can I combine steel weights across multiple diameters in one project?

Yes. Saving slab, beam, column, and footing steel calculations into a CivilCalc Pro project aggregates total steel requirements alongside concrete and formwork in a unified BOQ.
